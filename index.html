Ask us

Before your first visit.

What do memberships cost?

Memberships differ by location, so we confirm the right option with you directly. Call Howick on 021 023 50781 or email empireeastauckland@gmail.com and we will confirm the price for you — this page lists no invented prices.

When are classes?

Class times are on our timetable and can change, so confirm the current timetable with us before your first visit. Email or call your nearest location and we will tell you the next class that fits you.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Come as you are — classes are small-group and coached, so the instructor adapts the session to you. Hard work and showing up matter more than where you start.

Where are you?

Three Auckland locations: Howick (63 Union Road), Ōtara (6/100 Alexander Crescent) and Māngere (39B Rennie Drive). Howick is our East Auckland home.

What is Hauora?

Hauora is wellbeing beyond the workout — including SHIFT Nutrition and Ready Made Meals, so your progress continues outside class.
